**Behind the scenes.** [One short, specific story: a bug, a thing you cut, a number that surprised you. Yours to write; the sheets don't have it.]

**In the demo.** [What of this is in 0.3 and what waits for the full game. Fact sheet: Demo vs full game.]

Check before posting: every number is under Numbers on a sheet; nothing from a sheet's "Not ready to quote" line is promised; full-game footage is labeled.

**GoonCrusher now has a map: 30 levels, six regions**

The old demo had a list of levels. The new one has a road. You start in The Wilds and drive it stop by stop.

[Clip: `elements piece_road_map`, then `trailer_launch world_pullback`]

**What changed**
- Levels are stops on a road map, five to a region. The fifth is the region's finale.
- Each stop has its own poster, its own goons and its own layout: creeks and fords on Prairie Run, hedgerow lanes in Orchard Lanes, slot canyons in Red Canyon, pine thickets in Moose Woods.
- Every stop features three game modes. Win any one of them and the next stop opens. A finale has to be won on Medium.
- The world is generated and streams in as you drive.

**Why**
The 0.1 build had eight levels in a flat list. A list doesn't tell you where you are going. A road does.
