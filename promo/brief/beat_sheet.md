# Trailer beat sheet

The launch trailer, beat by beat. Each beat names the footage it needs: a shot in `promo/shots/trailer_launch.json`, a taped hand drive, or a piece from `elements.json`. Change the beats freely; then add or edit the matching shots so the whole trailer can be filmed again from this page.

Steam plays the first seconds of a trailer muted in search results and on hover, so open on gameplay, not a logo.
