# Steam update template

A Steam announcement for a demo update. Steam shows the title and the summary line in feeds, so both must work alone. Lead with a clip or a still, not a logo.

## Template

**Title:** [Game] [version]: [the biggest change in five words or fewer]

**Summary line:** [One sentence, under about 120 characters. Changelog: the entry's opening line.]

[Header image or clip. `promo/shots/steam_screens.json`, or the best shot named in `../README.md`.]

**Headlines**
[Five to eight bullets. Changelog: Headlines, copied and tightened.]

**Full changes**
[One short paragraph or three to five bullets per area, in the changelog's section order: World and levels, Modes, Cars and handling, Goons, Pickups and prize games, Progression and unlocks, HUD and menus, Radio and sound, Settings and performance, Controls and accessibility. Mark "(full game)" as the changelog does, or leave those lines out of a demo post.]

**Saves**
[Only when saves are affected. Changelog: the entry's opening line.]

**Known issues**
[Changelog: Known issues. Add what players have reported.]

**Thanks**
[Who played, reported or helped; where to send feedback. Yours to write.]

[Call to action: wishlist, and where to report bugs.]

## Example for 0.3 (DRAFT: rewrite in your own voice)

Built only from `CHANGELOG.md` and the fact sheets.

**Title:** GoonCrusher Demo 0.3: A Whole New Road

**Summary line:** Ten levels, 18 game modes, 44 goons, a radio station and a handbrake. The demo has been rebuilt.

[Clip: `trailer_launch cold_open` (Prairie Run, sedan)]

**Headlines**
- A road map in place of the level list. The demo has two regions, The Wilds and Tribe Country: ten levels.
- 18 game modes in three kinds: Crusher, Trial and the Goon Cup, where you race five rival drivers.
- A new cast of goons, each with one trick, a tell and a weak moment.
- A handbrake, drift boosts, a horn, and two signature traits per car.
- Gift boxes with prize games inside: Claw Crane, Hubcap Shuffle and Scratch Card.
- Pickups in unlock trees, with a gadget slot and a boost slot.
- GoonCrusher Radio: 14 original songs, a DJ, ads and idents.
- A dashboard for each car and a full settings menu with four quality presets.

**Full changes**
- **World and levels.** Fences, crates and hay break if you hit them fast enough. Log piles roll, beehives swarm, sluice gates flood. Water has depth. The Wilds has stampedes and flash floods.
- **Modes.** Every level plays Sprint, then Countdown, then three featured modes. Every mode has Easy, Medium and Hard, with bronze, silver and gold medals.
- **Cars and handling.** The demo's sedan, van and taxi each drive their own way. Damage has five systems and shows on the car.
- **Goons.** The demo's levels field 28 of them, from the Wild Things and the Goon Tribe. The Goonopedia keeps notes on every one you have crushed.
- **Progression.** Win any featured mode to open the next level. First clears pay a bonus.
- **HUD and menus.** A rear-view mirror for the clock and the goal, sun visors for the rest, and a new garage.
- **Settings.** Potato, Low, Medium and High presets, Render Resolution down to 540p, rebindable keys, and an Accessibility tab.

The full list is in the changelog: [link to CHANGELOG.md or a pasted copy].

Saves from the 0.1 demo do not carry over. 0.3 starts a new one and keeps a backup copy of the old file.

**Known issues**
- Nothing has had a hand-tuning pass yet: clocks, prices, payouts and mode targets will change.
- Night crowds can drop frames on low-end graphics.
